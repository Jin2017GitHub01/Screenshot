# Privacy Policy – Capture Everything

_Last updated: October 18, 2026_

Capture Everything ("the app") is a screen capture utility for Windows.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry,
no ads, and makes no network connections.

## How the app handles your screenshots

- Screenshots are captured and processed **only on your device**.
- A capture is placed on the Windows clipboard so you can paste it.
- A capture is saved to disk **only when you choose Save**, at the location you pick.
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Third parties

The app does not include third-party SDKs and does not send data to third parties.
If you paste or share a screenshot into another app, that app's privacy policy applies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

# Privacy Policy – Capture Everything

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Capture Everything ("the app") is a screen, video, and system-audio capture utility for Windows.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry,
no ads, and makes no network connections.

## How the app handles your screenshots

- Screenshots are captured and processed **only on your device**.
- A capture is placed on the Windows clipboard so you can paste it.
- A capture is saved to disk **only when you choose Save**, at the location you pick.
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## How the app handles video and audio recordings

- Screen video and system audio are recorded **only while you have started a recording**.
  Only the screen region you select and the sound your computer is playing (system audio)
  are captured. The microphone and webcam are not used.
- While recording, data is written to a temporary file on your device, which is
  deleted as soon as the recording is saved or discarded.
- A recording is saved **only when you choose Save**, at the location you pick.
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Third parties

The app uses the open-source [NAudio](https://github.com/naudio/NAudio) library for
audio capture, which runs entirely on your device and sends no data anywhere.
The app does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not
send data to third parties.
If you paste or share a screenshot or recording into another app, that app's privacy policy applies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
